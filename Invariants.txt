Varying Invariants:
    Second train approaches during alarm ringing either north or south.
        approach signal will always precede a depart signal
    north and south arrive at the same time, one departs sooner than the other
        approach signal will always precede a depart signal
    Train stops in intersection and reverses
        Trains on each track only run in one direction
        A train that arrives will eventually depart on the same rail
    Careless well digger cuts the power line to the system
        The power will never be interupted 
    Runaway train that speeds faster than approach timer
        The alarm timer is short enough to precede the trains physical arrival at crossing
